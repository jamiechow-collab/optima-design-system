## Types

There are five types of badges: Info (default), Message, Positive, Error, Warning.

  <TypesExample />
</Unstyled>

## Sizes

There are three sizes: sm, md, lg. Leading and trailing icons scale with the badge — 12px on sm
and md, 16px on lg.

<Unstyled>
  <SizesExample />
</Unstyled>

## Variants

A badge can optionally show a leading icon, a trailing icon, or both — common icons would be
dots, checkmarks, close, or arrows.
