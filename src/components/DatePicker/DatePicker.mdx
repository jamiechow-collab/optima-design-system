## Overview

Use **Date Field** if the date can be remembered by the user easily, such as a date of birth, and
they don't need a calendar to anticipate the dates — it consists only of an input field. Use
**Date Picker** — a calendar picker (single or range) — when the user needs to know a date's
relationship to other days, or when a date could be variable; the user can view and pick dates
from a calendar widget.

```tsx
<DatePicker mode="single" value={value} onChange={setValue} label="Field title" />
<DatePicker mode="range" value={[start, end]} onChange={setRange} label="Field title" />
<DateField value={value} onChange={setValue} label="Date of birth" />
```

## Anatomy

<Unstyled>
  <AnatomyExample />
</Unstyled>

The Calendar is made up of a **Month and Year control** (header, with prev/next navigation and
jump-to menus), a **Week number** column (optional), the date grid itself, and an optional
**Action button** row (Cancel/Apply) at the foot of the popover.

- The day grid always renders 6 full weeks (42 cells) so the calendar's height never shifts
  between months.
- Days outside the currently-viewed month are shown muted, matching the Disabled visual style.
- Today is marked with a small dot under its number.

## Types

<Unstyled>
  <TypesExample />
</Unstyled>

**Date Field** — a typed-only `DD / MM / YYYY` field. No calendar icon or popover by default.
Clicking a segment (day/month/year) jumps the caret there; typing auto-advances to the next
segment.

**Date Picker (single date)** — a field that opens a Calendar popover. Selecting a date fills the
field and closes the calendar immediately.

**Date Picker (range)** — the same field/calendar pair in range mode. The first click sets the
range start; the second sets the end and shows a continuous highlighted band between them, then
closes the calendar.

## States

Each field has Default, Hover, Active (calendar open, for Date Picker), Focused (keyboard focus,
shows a focus ring), and Disabled states.

## Interactions

**Date Field — mouse** — clicking anywhere in the field starts typing in the first (day) segment;
clicking directly on a segment jumps the caret there. Clicking outside the field auto-saves the
entry. Clicking back into an already-filled field re-highlights the first segment for easy
overwrite.

**Date Field — keyboard** — focusing the field highlights the first segment. **Tab** (or **Arrow
Left/Right**) moves between segments. **Esc** removes focus from the field.

**Date Picker — mouse** — clicking the field (or its calendar icon) opens the calendar, anchored
below the field. Hovering a date previews it; clicking selects it. In single mode, selecting a
date closes the calendar immediately. In range mode, the first click sets the start, hovering
previews the range, and the second click sets the end and closes the calendar. Clicking the
Month or Year label opens a menu to jump directly to a month/year without closing the calendar.

**Date Picker — keyboard** — focusing the field (e.g. via **Tab**) automatically opens the
calendar. From there, **Tab** moves through the prev-month chevron, the Month label, the Year
label, the next-month chevron, and then into the date grid. **Arrow keys** move focus by day/week,
**Home/End** jump to the start/end of the week, **Page Up/Down** move by month (**Shift** + Page
Up/Down by year), and **Space** or **Enter** selects the focused date. **Esc** closes the calendar
and returns focus to the field.
