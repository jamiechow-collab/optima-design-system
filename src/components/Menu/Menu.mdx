## Overview

Typically, this context is determined by the user's current selection prior to invoking the menu.
Menus can be opened from components such as menu buttons or through right-clicking.

This guidance will help you understand how to format, structure, and use the menu component
effectively.

<Unstyled>
  <OverviewExample />
</Unstyled>

**When to use**

**Declutter interface** — In general, use menus to hide less frequently used or advanced options
until users specifically need them. This keeps the interface clean and focused on essential
elements.

**Context menu** — Context menus are triggered by a right-click interaction. A context menu is a
defined list of options in a menu that appears above all other content on a page and is related
to the selected content or element it was triggered from.

**Customisable actions** — Menus can speed up interactions for advanced users who are already
familiar with the application. These users often rely on shortcuts and context-specific actions to
work more efficiently.

**When not to use**

**Form submission** — When submitting a form with a static list of options for users to choose
from, or when filtering, use a dropdown instead of a menu.

## Types

There are three types of Menu item. Single line contains only one line of label, which should be
used by default.

Only when more context is needed for categories or explanation of the item, then you can consider
one with a title or with a description.

## Behaviour

Menu item label should NOT be truncated. Labels should be easy to scan and understandable for
users.

Only under certain circumstances, when labels are not in our control (e.g. user-generated
content), a label can be truncated, but should show a tooltip when a user hovers over the item.
For a Menu Item that is not single line, it can be truncated when it gets to the end of the second
line.

## Alignment

**Default** — By default, a combo button, menu button, or overflow button remains positioned at
the top, to the left or right side of the menu when it is open, depending on the available space
and layout.

**Alternatives** — Alternatively, open menus can be positioned at the bottom, to the left or right
of its associated menu button trigger, depending on the available space and layout.

## Interactions

**Mouse**

Once the menu opens by clicking on a menu button trigger or right-clicking on an area, users can:

- Select or deselect an item by clicking on it
- Hover over the item with the caret icon to reveal additional options or actions from that item
- Click outside of the menu area to close it

**Keyboard**

Once the menu opens by pressing **Return** or **Enter** on the menu trigger, users can:

- Press **Up** and **Down arrows** to navigate between menu options
- Press **Return** or **Enter** on an item to select or deselect it
- Press **Right Arrow** on the item with the chevron icon to reveal the sub-menu
- When a sub-menu is triggered, tap **Arrow Up** and **Down** to navigate
- Press **Left Arrow** to leave the sub-menu and return to the main menu
- Press **Escape** to close it
