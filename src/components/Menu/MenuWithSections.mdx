## Overview

Especially if the menu is too long, sections help users get a quick sense of what content to look
for.

## Content

**Ordering and grouping**

To aid discoverability, arrange menu items in a meaningful sequence that aligns with the order of
operations. To help users focus on the most relevant options, place the ones used most often at
the top.

When there is a significant amount of menu items, it's advisable to group them into sections,
separated by divider lines.

**Number of items within a menu**

Expansive lists of options in menus can overwhelm users. With context menu and overflow menu, try
to keep the list length manageable by including no more than 12 items to help users scan through
items easily without having to scroll.

If there are lots of items, try to break it into a separate menu with a different trigger, or use
sections to divide the content.
