## Overview

A submenu is a menu item with the chevron icon on the right, offering additional related
commands. While submenus can help tidy up a context menu and make its options clearer,
introducing multiple levels of submenus can make the user experience more complex and harder to
navigate. Avoid multiple levels of nesting when it comes to submenus.

**Actions associated with a shared label**

When a term appears in more than two menu items in the same group, consider eliminating the
recurring term in the submenu label to help people predict its content and scan easily — see the
"Shared Label Removed" story, where "Export as PDF/CSV/PNG" becomes just "PDF/CSV/PNG" once
nested under an "Export as..." trigger.

## Behaviour

A menu item with a submenu shows a chevron on the right in place of a checkmark or shortcut. The
submenu opens on click, on hover, or by pressing **Enter**, **Space**, or the **Right Arrow** key
while the item has focus. It closes on **Left Arrow**, **Escape**, or a click outside the menu.

## Interactions

**Mouse**

Hover over the item with the chevron icon to reveal additional options or actions from that item.
Click outside of the menu area to close it.

**Keyboard**

- Press **Right Arrow** on the item with the chevron icon to reveal the sub-menu
- When the sub-menu is triggered, press **Arrow Up** and **Down** to navigate
- Press **Left Arrow** to leave the sub-menu and return to the main menu
- Press **Escape** to close it
