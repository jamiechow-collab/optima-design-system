## Overview

Progress bars communicate how far along a task or process is, without requiring the user to take
any action. They're display-only — there's no hover, focus, or disabled state.

## Anatomy

<Unstyled>
  <AnatomyExample />
</Unstyled>

A progress bar is made up of an optional field title with tooltip, and a track — an optional
leading or trailing "n%" label, and the bar itself (a filled pill that grows from empty to full
width as `value` increases from 0 to 100).

- The field title and tooltip are optional.
- The leading and trailing percentage labels are both optional and independent — use whichever
  reads best in context.
- Always pass an accessible name — either a visible `label`, or an `aria-label` when the bar has
  no visible title.
