## Overview

The slider lets users pick a range between two bounds by dragging either handle. It's built from
two overlaid native range inputs, so both handles are independently keyboard-accessible and
draggable, and each handle can never cross the other.

## Anatomy

<Unstyled>
  <AnatomyExample />
</Unstyled>

A slider is made up of a track (the light grey base), a fill (the dark segment between the two
handles), two draggable handles, and an optional pair of labels showing each handle's current
value.

- Labels are optional — only show them when the exact value matters to the user.
- Handles can be pushed together but never crossed — dragging the low handle past the high handle
  (or vice versa) clamps it instead.

## Values

The fill always spans between the two handles, however close together or far apart they are.

## Interactions

**Mouse** — Users can drag either handle along the track. Clicking a handle focuses it.

**Keyboard** — Users can press **Tab** to move focus to a handle, then use the **Arrow keys**
(or **Home**/**End**) to adjust its value in `step` increments.
