## Overview

The spinner is a small, continuously rotating indicator that tells users something is loading —
use it wherever a wait state has no determinate progress to show (see [Progress
Bar](?path=/docs/components-progress-bar--docs) for a determinate alternative).

## Sizes

There are six sizes. xs (16px) is the default and most commonly used, e.g. inside a Button.

<Unstyled>
  <SizesExample />
</Unstyled>

## Types

There are two types — Primary is a dark arc for light backgrounds, Secondary is a white arc for
dark or coloured backgrounds.

<Unstyled>
  <TypesExample />
</Unstyled>

## Application: inside a Button

`Button`'s `loading` prop swaps its leading icon for a `Spinner`, automatically picking Primary or
Secondary to match the button's variant.
