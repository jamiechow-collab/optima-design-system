## Overview

Toggles are used for a single, independent on/off setting that takes effect immediately —
unlike a checkbox, there's no separate "confirm" step. Each toggle works independently from any
others on the page.

Examples of usage include settings screens and preference panels (e.g. "Dark Mode",
"Notifications").

## Interactions

**Mouse**

Users can flip the switch by clicking it directly or by clicking its label. Having both regions
interactive creates a more accessible click target. The only hover effect when the mouse is
placed over the target is a pointer shape change.

**Keyboard**

Users can navigate to and between toggles by pressing **Tab** or **Shift-Tab**. Users can flip the
focused toggle by pressing **Space**.
