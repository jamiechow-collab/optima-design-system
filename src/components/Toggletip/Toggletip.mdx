## Overview

`Toggletip` wraps a single trigger (must be a real interactive element, e.g. a `<button>`, so
Enter/Space activation works natively) and shows a popover on click — unlike
[Tooltip](?path=/docs/components-tooltips--docs), it can hold interactive content such as an
action button, and stays open until the user dismisses it.

```tsx
<Toggletip
  title="Rich Tooltips Title"
  description="More explanation or description about the the tips or information for the tooltips"
  actionLabel="Action"
  onAction={() => {}}
>
  <button aria-label="More info">
    <Icon name="info-with-circle" />
  </button>
</Toggletip>
```

## Anatomy

**Trigger** — any component with an integrated toggletip: commonly an info icon, a button, or
text.

**Toggletip** — a container holding text and interactive elements (e.g. an action button). The
popover has no fixed width — its description wraps at a max-width of 250px, and the title stays
on a single line, so the popover is exactly as wide as its widest line, up to that 250px.

## Variants

There are two colour variants — Primary (dark) is the default; Secondary (light) suits dark or
busy backgrounds.

## Placement

Toggletips can be used on various parts of the UI, including but not limited to headers, data
tables, side panels, modals, and read-only cards. When active, the popover may be positioned top,
bottom, left, or right of the trigger.

## Interactions

**Mouse** — Toggletips are triggered on click, and are dismissed by clicking again on the trigger
or anywhere outside the active popover or trigger.

**Keyboard** — Toggletips are triggered by pressing **Enter** or **Space** while the trigger has
focus. Users can dismiss a toggletip by pressing **Esc**, which also returns focus to the trigger.
