## Overview

`Tooltip` wraps a single trigger element (it must accept a ref/forward focus and hover events,
e.g. a `Button`, an `Icon` wrapped in a focusable element, or a native element) and shows its
content on hover or keyboard focus. Tooltips never contain interactive content — for a
click-triggered popover with an action, see [Toggletip](?path=/docs/components-toggletips--docs).

```tsx
<Tooltip label="Brief description of a term">
  <Icon name="info-with-circle" />
</Tooltip>
```

**Tooltip** — a container holding a short or brief text.

## Alignment

The tooltip's container may be aligned to start, center, or end (relative to the trigger) to keep
it from bleeding off the page or covering important information.

## Placement

Tooltip direction defaults to `auto`: on open, the tooltip detects the edges of the browser and
flips between top and bottom to stay in view. It can instead be pinned to a specific side — right,
left, bottom, or top — of the trigger. Don't cover related content essential to the user's task;
tooltips shouldn't bleed off the page or behind other content.

## Behaviour

There is a maximum width of 200px applied for helping with readability — if the label or
description goes really long, it wraps within that width rather than overflowing or forcing the
bubble wider.

## Interactions

**Mouse** — Tooltips are triggered when the mouse hovers over the trigger, and persist while the
mouse remains over the tooltip or the trigger. They're dismissed by hovering away.

Definition tooltips can use either hover or click interactions depending on the situation — hover
for a quick glance, click when the tooltip needs more time to read or might otherwise be triggered
unintentionally (see [Toggletip](?path=/docs/components-toggletips--docs) for the click-based
version).

**Keyboard** — Users can navigate to the trigger and reveal the tooltip by pressing **Tab**. It's
dismissed by pressing **Esc**, or automatically once focus moves away.
